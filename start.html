Get started with Long Run Hybrid Coach

You need two things: a free Intervals.icu account, and Claude or ChatGPT. The coach reads the training data already sitting in that account.

No Intervals.icu account yet? That's fine — the steps below take you to its sign-in page, where you can create one with Google in about 30 seconds.

Claude works on its free plan. ChatGPT currently needs a Business, Enterprise, or Edu workspace — on a personal plan, wait for this coach to appear in the ChatGPT directory.

Connect in four steps

Remote MCP URL https://mcp.paceandstaystrong.com/mcp
  1. Open your AI's connector settings.

    Look for the option to add a new connector.

    claude.ai or Claude Desktop: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.

    ChatGPT: full read/write connector access is in beta for Business, Enterprise, and Edu workspaces on ChatGPT web via Apps/developer mode. Everyone else: wait for this to appear in the ChatGPT directory.

  2. Paste in the address.

    Paste the address above into the remote MCP server URL field, then continue. MCP is just the standard way AI tools connect to outside services — you're only pasting in a link.

  3. Approve access in Intervals.icu.

    Sign in when Intervals.icu asks for your permission. Check all four boxes, then approve. Each box covers a different kind of access. Skip one, and the feature that needs it breaks in a way that's hard to trace back — check all four for the full coaching flow.

    See exactly what you're approving, and how to revoke it later.

  4. Go back to your AI and start talking.

    Make sure the connection is turned on for this conversation, then ask the coach to read your recent training.

Try: “Read my recent training and help me set a 28-day running and strength direction.”

Is your Intervals.icu account new or empty?

The coach automatically reads what's already in Intervals.icu. If your account is new, here are two ways to get your history in first:

  • Connect a device or app you already use. In Intervals.icu's own Settings, connect Garmin or whatever watch or training app you already have. Past activity shows up automatically — once it's there, ask the coach to read your training again.
  • Send your history straight to the coach. Drop a CSV, an Apple Health export, or a .fit file into the conversation, and ask the coach to import it. (The file goes to the coach, not into Intervals.icu.)

What happens next

The coach reads your training data in Intervals.icu and talks through where you're at. If it suggests a workout for your calendar, you'll see the whole thing before anything gets written. A successful delivery means Intervals.icu saved the workout and the coach read it back to confirm. It doesn't mean your watch has it yet.
