Support

Last updated: 11 September 2026 · A personal GitHub project, not a company, maintained by an independent developer in Taiwan.

Start with the connection checklist below. You can ask the coach to export, correct or delete your data — and if that does not go through, ask for the export or deletion by email instead.

Connection and first-use help

  • Cannot find “Add connector”? Check the supported AI plans. Claude Free allows one custom connector; team accounts may also need administrator approval.
  • Added it, but the coach cannot read data? Check that the connector is connected and enabled in this conversation. If a permission is missing, reconnect and review the four Intervals.icu permissions.
  • Connected, but no training history? Check for activities in Intervals.icu itself. You can start by describing your training and goals; see the empty-account guide.
  • Sent a workout, but it is not on your watch? Check the Intervals.icu calendar first, then your device sync settings. Calendar acceptance does not confirm arrival on the watch.

In your email, name the AI you use, the step that failed and the error message, with private data removed. Never send passwords, API keys or authorization tokens.

1. Manage your data in conversation

  • Get a copy of your data. Ask the coach what it holds about you. The archive arrives in the conversation and states what it deliberately leaves out.
  • Delete your account. Ask the coach to delete it. You see a preview of exactly what goes and what deletion cannot reach, and nothing is removed until you confirm that preview.
  • Correct something you reported. Report the corrected value. For the records where only the newest one counts — a body measurement for one day, or one exercise on one day — the new value replaces the standing one, and the coach tells you what it displaced. Your plan’s own version history is separate: it is only ever added to, never edited in place, so correcting a record does not quietly rewrite what the plan said last week.
  • Retract something you reported. Ask the coach to retract the relevant athlete-reported record when the right result is removal rather than replacement.
  • Stop all access. Revoke Long Run Hybrid Coach in Intervals.icu. That ends future reads and writes; it does not by itself delete what is already held. If you want your data deleted too, delete it first — your Intervals.icu sign-in is what identifies you here, so once it is revoked you may have to re-authorize briefly just to finish the deletion (section 6).

Your connection is the identity check, which is why none of these needs a separate support request. If your AI client blocks the call before it reaches this service, ask by email instead — it does not depend on a tool call going through.

2. Reaching a person, and asking for your data by email

Email: tingcctwai@gmail.com. It reaches the developer directly and it is private, so it is the right place for anything involving your health data, your training, or your account. The public issue tracker is for bugs and feature requests that other people benefit from seeing — read the next section before you post there.

If the coach cannot export or delete your data, ask here and it will be done for you. Some AI clients refuse a tool call at their own approval step before it reaches this service; the conversation can then neither finish the request nor explain why. This route does not depend on a tool call:

  1. Write to tingcctwai@gmail.com, say whether you want a copy of your data or its deletion, and include your Intervals.icu athlete id — the number in your own Intervals.icu URL.
  2. You get a reply asking you to re-authorize Long Run Hybrid Coach at Intervals.icu: reconnect it in the AI client you use, then reply to say you have. That is the same consent you gave when you first connected — it changes nothing and deletes nothing.
  3. Re-authorizing is what shows the account is yours. An athlete id, a display name, or the address an email came from are not proof on their own, so none of them is enough by itself. You will never be asked for a password, an API key, or an authorization token.
  4. For a copy: the archive is sent only to the address that made the request. For a deletion: you are sent the exact scope first, nothing is removed until you confirm that scope, and the reply afterwards carries the receipt.

This is a personal project with one maintainer. There is no guaranteed response time and none is promised here. Anything you can do yourself, above, is faster than waiting.

If you think you have found a security or privacy vulnerability, do not describe it publicly. Email it instead.

3. What must never go in a public issue

The issue tracker is public and permanent. It must stay usable without you disclosing anything, so do not post — and you will never be asked for:

  • health, training, or plan content, including screenshots of it;
  • an Intervals.icu athlete id, an owner id, an access token, or a token fingerprint;
  • passwords, OAuth credentials, or API keys of any kind.

What is enough to identify your account is the opaque account reference printed in your own data export. It is safe to quote in public, cannot be turned back into an account id, and is all an operator needs to find you.

4. What support can and cannot do

  • Export and deletion in the conversation take effect immediately, because you perform them. By email they take as long as the reply does — see the response time above.
  • Deletion reaches the whole account and every snapshot stored beside it, in one operation.
  • Deletion does not reach workouts already written to your Intervals.icu calendar, and does not withdraw your Intervals.icu authorization. Both are yours to remove.
  • What you reported yourself can be corrected or retracted through the coach. The plan’s own version and decision history is different: it is only added to, never rewritten by hand.
  • No backup-retention window is promised, because none has been measured. This product creates no routine backups.

5. If deletion was refused

Three reasons are documented, and you can clear all of them yourself. If none of them is what you were told — or your AI client refused the call before it reached this service — ask by email instead.

  • A delivery has not finished. Intervals.icu may hold a workout the product has not reconciled. Retry the same delivery, or check your calendar and clear the attempt, then delete.
  • The account changed since the preview. You reported a lift, or a session reconciled, between preview and confirmation. Preview again.
  • Your store is being moved. An operator is migrating it. Preview again after the migration completes.

6. If you can no longer connect

If you revoked your Intervals.icu authorization or lost that account, the identity check went with it. There is no proof of ownership this product can evaluate — every identifier it holds is either a one-way digest or a value anyone could claim — so an account will not be deleted on an assertion. Re-authorizing at Intervals.icu even briefly resolves it — in the conversation, or by email, which needs the same re-authorization and nothing else.

7. Not medical support

Long Run Hybrid Coach is a training-planning tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor anything, and this page is not a medical channel. Pain, illness, or unusual symptoms are for qualified medical care, not for the coach and not for an issue.
