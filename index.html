Train for today. Stay capable for the long run.

Long Run Hybrid Coach is an Intervals-first, device-agnostic coach for athletes who run and lift. It maintains one current 28-day direction from the training evidence you already sync, then writes only workouts you explicitly approve.

Get started See how it works

One current plan for running and strength.

Training changes when life changes. A missed run, a completed lifting session, or incomplete recovery evidence should affect what comes next. Long Run Hybrid Coach reconciles planned work against identity-backed activities and updates one current direction.

1

One current direction

The next 28 days and the current week change together. There is no folder of stale plans to reconcile by hand.

2

Run and lift together

Running and strength share the same week and recovery budget. Strength is part of the plan, not an afterthought.

3

Revise from actual evidence

Completed activities and available wellness signals inform revisions. Missing or stale evidence remains unknown—not zero.

Strength stays specific.

“Back squat, 4×5 at 100 kg” is a plan entry, not a note you left yourself. Intervals.icu receives the session title; the movement, sets, reps and load stay in the conversation, where you can read them back any time.

How it works

Devices and apps feed Intervals.icu — richness varies by device, but the coaching path stays the same. The coach reads that evidence together with anything you've told it directly.

Before writing You see the full workout and approve it.

Calendar permission allows a proposal. Nothing is written until you confirm the exact workout.

After writing Intervals.icu is read back.

A successful delivery means Intervals.icu accepted and returned the workout. It does not prove that a watch or another device received it.

Questions before you connect

What do I need to start?

An Intervals.icu account and an AI assistant that lets you add a remote MCP connector by URL. The start page gives you the endpoint and the four setup steps. If you don't have an Intervals.icu account yet, it's free — the connection flow reaches its sign-in page, where you can create one with Google in about 30 seconds.

Is your Intervals.icu account new or empty?

The coach only reads what Intervals.icu already has. Connect a device or app you already use in Intervals.icu's own Settings — Garmin or your existing watch or training app account — and past activity backfills automatically; or send the coach a CSV, an Apple Health export, or a .fit file directly in the conversation and ask it to import your history.

Do I need a particular watch?

No. The coach works from the completed activities and available wellness evidence that reach your Intervals.icu account.

Can I run my own gateway instead of the hosted one?

Yes. Self-hosting runs the same product surface with your own Intervals.icu OAuth application credentials and your own state, for developers who want to operate it themselves. Most people should use the hosted endpoint on the start page.

Why do all four Intervals.icu permissions need to be selected?

The permissions let the coach read completed activities, read available wellness data, read and write the training calendar, and read Run settings. A missing threshold pace can be filled only inside a workout you already confirmed. Leaving one permission unticked does not make the connection safer; it makes one capability fail in a way that is hard to trace back.

Can the coach write a workout without my approval?

No. Every workout is shown to you in full and written only after you confirm that exact proposal.

What does a successful delivery mean?

It means Intervals.icu accepted and returned the workout. It does not prove that a watch or another device received it.

How do I revoke access or delete my data?

Remove this application in your Intervals.icu settings to stop future reads and writes. Because authorization is granted per application, that stops access for every AI assistant you connected. Revoking does not delete data already held. To delete that data, ask the coach in the conversation; it shows you what will be removed before you confirm. Workouts already on your Intervals.icu calendar remain yours to keep or remove. The support page has more detail.

Ready to connect?

Add the remote MCP connector, approve access in Intervals.icu, and start with your recent training evidence.
