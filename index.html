Train for today. Stay capable for the long run.

Long Run Hybrid Coach is a coach for runners who also lift, built around Intervals.icu — it works with whatever device you already use. It keeps one 28-day plan based on your training data, and only writes a workout after you approve it.

Get started See how it works

One current plan for running and strength.

Life changes, so training should too. A missed run, a lifting session you actually finished, recovery data that's incomplete — all of that should shape what's next. Long Run Hybrid Coach checks the plan against what you actually did, and updates your one current direction.

1

One current direction

The next 28 days and the current week change together. There is no folder of stale plans to reconcile by hand.

2

Run and lift together

Running and strength share the same week and recovery budget. Strength is part of the plan, not an afterthought.

3

Revise from what really happened

It looks at what you actually did, and whatever recovery data is available, before changing anything. If something's missing or out of date, the plan treats it as unknown — not as zero.

Strength stays specific.

“Back squat, 4×5 at 100 kg” is a plan entry, not a note you left yourself. Intervals.icu receives the session title; the movement, sets, reps and load stay in the conversation, where you can read them back any time.

How it works

Your devices and apps send data to Intervals.icu — how much detail you get depends on the device, but the coaching itself works the same either way. The coach reads that training data, plus anything you tell it directly.

Before writing You see the full workout and approve it.

Calendar permission means the coach can propose a workout — it can't write anything until you confirm the exact workout you saw.

After writing The coach checks back with Intervals.icu.

A successful delivery means Intervals.icu accepted and saved the workout. It doesn't prove your watch has received it yet.

Questions before you connect

What do I need to start?

An Intervals.icu account, and Claude or ChatGPT. The start page has the address to paste in and all four setup steps. If you don't have an Intervals.icu account yet, it's free — the setup flow takes you to its sign-in page, where you can create one with Google in about 30 seconds.

Is your Intervals.icu account new or empty?

The coach only sees what's already in Intervals.icu. You can connect a device or app you already use — Garmin or another watch or training app — in Intervals.icu's own Settings, and past activity shows up automatically. Or just send the coach a CSV, an Apple Health export, or a .fit file in the conversation, and ask it to import your history.

Do I need a particular watch?

No. The coach works from whatever completed activities and wellness data end up in your Intervals.icu account.

Can I run my own gateway instead of the hosted one?

Yes. Self-hosting runs the exact same product with your own Intervals.icu OAuth credentials and your own storage — it's for developers who want to run it themselves. Most people should just use the hosted version on the start page.

Why do all four Intervals.icu permissions need to be checked?

The four permissions let the coach read your completed activities, read your wellness data, read and write your training calendar, and read your Run settings. Your Run settings hold your threshold pace — if it's missing, the coach can only fill it in through a workout you've already confirmed. Skipping one box doesn't make the connection safer. It just makes one feature quietly break in a way that's hard to trace back.

Can the coach write a workout without my approval?

No. Every workout is shown to you in full first, and it's only written after you confirm exactly what you saw.

What does a successful delivery mean?

It means Intervals.icu accepted the workout and confirmed it back. It doesn't mean your watch has received it yet.

How do I revoke access or delete my data?

Remove this application in your Intervals.icu settings to stop future reads and writes. Because Intervals.icu grants access per application, not per AI, that disconnects every AI you'd connected — Claude, ChatGPT, all of them. Revoking doesn't delete data that's already there. To delete that, ask the coach in the conversation — it'll show you what's being removed before you confirm. Workouts already on your Intervals.icu calendar stay yours to keep or remove. More detail on the support page.

Ready to connect?

Connect it, approve access in Intervals.icu, and start with your recent training data.
