Your first message

Read my recent training, then ask about my goals and available time each week. Help me plan running and strength.

How do I know it worked? The coach should report the training it could read, or clearly say the account has no data yet. Then discuss your goals and schedule. If you only get general advice, ask it to check whether it used the connector.

Can I start with a new or empty Intervals.icu account?

Yes. Tell the coach how you have been training, how much time you have and what you want to achieve. You do not need to fill in your history first.

Later, connect a supported device or app in Intervals.icu Settings. How much history imports depends on the source. Once activities appear in Intervals.icu, ask the coach to read them again.

Questions before you connect

What does a strength workout include?

The plan can retain exercises, sets, reps and loads for you to read back in conversation. Intervals.icu receives the session title; the calendar or watch is not the full strength prescription.

Is this an app I install?

No. It runs inside a conversation with Claude or ChatGPT — you connect it once, then you just talk to it. There is nothing to download, no separate account to create here, and no dashboard to keep checking. Your plan lives in the coach and your workouts land in your Intervals.icu calendar.

What do I need to start?

An Intervals.icu account and an AI account with connector access. Start with Claude; its free plan allows one custom connector. The full ChatGPT flow currently needs Business, Enterprise or Edu on the web and workspace approval. See the setup steps.

Does this cost anything?

The coach is currently free, with no paid tier. Intervals.icu offers free accounts, and you can start on Claude’s free plan. Your AI platform’s usage limits and subscription fees still apply. This is an early independent project; availability may change.

Do I need a particular watch?

No particular watch is required, and you can use the coach without one. It uses records in Intervals.icu and training you report in conversation. Receiving workouts on a watch needs a compatible device and separate sync setup.

Can I run my own gateway instead of the hosted one?

Yes. Self-hosting runs the exact same product with your own Intervals.icu OAuth credentials and your own storage — it's for developers who want to run it themselves. Most people should just use the hosted address in the setup steps.

Why do all four Intervals.icu permissions need to be checked?

They cover completed activities, wellness data, your training calendar, and athlete settings. Settings writes are limited to filling a missing threshold pace during a delivery you already confirmed; an existing value is not overwritten. Calendar workouts still require your approval. Reconnect to grant a permission you missed.

Can the coach write a workout without my approval?

No. Every workout is shown to you in full first, and it's only written after you confirm exactly what you saw.

What does a successful delivery mean?

A successful delivery means Intervals.icu saved the workout and the coach read it back to confirm. It doesn't mean your watch has it yet.

How do I revoke access or delete my data?

To stop access: remove this application in your Intervals.icu settings. That ends future reads and writes. Because Intervals.icu grants access per application rather than per AI, removing it disconnects every AI you had connected — Claude, ChatGPT, all of them.

To delete your data as well: that one is an email request, not something the conversation does — the support page has the steps. Ask the coach to delete everything and it hands you that page; nothing is submitted and nothing removed until you write. The order does not matter: your athlete id finds the account whether or not you have already revoked access.

Workouts already on your Intervals.icu calendar stay yours to keep or remove either way. More detail on the support page.
