The difference

Three things a general AI chat cannot do.

Ask any AI for a training week and you will get a reasonable-looking one. Here is what changes when the coach is connected to your own training.

1

It sees what you actually trained

Your finished runs and lifts arrive from your own Intervals.icu account. The coach plans from those — not from whatever you happen to remember and type in.

2

Running and strength in the same week

One plan holds both, so the hard run and the heavy session are kept apart. Two separate plans cannot do that — neither knows what the other put on Tuesday.

3

It adjusts, instead of starting over

Missed a session, or Thursday disappeared? Come back and change this plan. It still knows what you have already done, so you are not handed a brand-new generic week.

Your first conversation

Four steps, about five minutes.

You need an Intervals.icu account — a brand-new empty one is fine — and a desktop browser for this first setup.

  1. Start in Claude.

    Claude’s free plan allows one custom connector, which is all this needs. Open Claude’s connector settings.

    I use ChatGPT

    The full coaching flow currently needs Business, Enterprise or Edu on the web, with custom MCP read-and-write access enabled by your workspace. Follow ChatGPT’s setup instructions to add a custom app through Apps / developer mode. Read-and-fetch access alone cannot save plans or send workouts, so on a personal plan start with Claude.

  2. Add Long Run Hybrid Coach.

    In Claude: Customize → Connectors → “+” → Add custom connector. Name it Long Run Hybrid Coach, and paste this address into the server URL field.

    Connection address https://mcp.paceandstaystrong.com/mcp
    Is there anything else to fill in?

    No. There is no API key and no OAuth credential to enter — the address is the whole of it. On a team account, an administrator may have to enable custom connectors before the option appears.

  3. Sign in to Intervals.icu and approve access.

    Choose Connect, sign in with your own account, then select all four permissions before approving. What the four permissions cover. If you missed one, reconnect and grant it.

  4. Go back to the conversation and start.

    Turn Long Run Hybrid Coach on in the conversation’s connector or tools menu — in Claude, “+” beside the message box → Connectors — then send this:

    Read my recent training, then ask about my goals and available time each week. Help me plan running and strength.

How do I know it worked? The coach should report the training it could read, or clearly say the account has no data yet. If you only get general advice, ask it to check whether it used the connector.

Can I start with a new or empty Intervals.icu account?

Yes. Tell the coach how you have been training, how much time you have and what you want to achieve. You do not need to fill in your history first.

Later, connect a supported device or app in Intervals.icu Settings. How much history imports depends on the source. Once activities appear in Intervals.icu, ask the coach to read them again.

Stuck? Try the connection checklist. For current menus and plan availability, see Claude and ChatGPT (checked 18 September 2026).

Common questions

What does a strength workout include?

The plan can retain exercises, sets, reps and loads for you to read back in conversation. Intervals.icu receives the session title; the calendar or watch is not the full strength prescription.

Is this an app I install?

No. It runs inside a conversation with Claude or ChatGPT: you connect it once, then you talk to it. There is nothing to download and no separate account to create here. Your plan lives in the coach, and your workouts land in your Intervals.icu calendar.

What do I need to start?

Nothing, if you only want to try it — that needs no account. To connect your own training you need an Intervals.icu account and an AI account with connector access. Start with Claude; its free plan allows one custom connector. The full ChatGPT flow currently needs Business, Enterprise or Edu on the web and workspace approval. Go to Get started.

Does this cost anything?

The coach is currently free, with no paid tier. Intervals.icu offers free accounts, and you can start on Claude’s free plan. Your AI platform’s usage limits and subscription fees still apply.

Do I need a particular watch?

No particular watch is required, and you can use the coach without one. It uses records in Intervals.icu and training you report in conversation.

To follow a workout on your watch: confirm it is in Intervals.icu, then use your device’s own sync. The coach can check that Intervals.icu received it; whether it reached the watch is something only the watch shows.

Can I run my own gateway instead of the hosted one?

Yes. Self-hosting runs the exact same product with your own Intervals.icu OAuth credentials and your own storage. Most people should use the hosted address under Get started.

Why do all four Intervals.icu permissions need to be checked?

They cover completed activities, wellness data, your training calendar, and athlete settings. Settings writes are limited to filling a missing threshold pace during a delivery you already confirmed; an existing value is not overwritten. Calendar workouts still require your approval. Reconnect to grant a permission you missed.

Can the coach write a workout without my approval?

No. Every workout is shown to you in full first, and what gets written is what you confirmed.

What does a successful delivery mean?

A successful delivery means Intervals.icu saved the workout and the coach read it back to confirm. It doesn't mean your watch has it yet.

How do I revoke access or delete my data?

To stop access: remove this application in your Intervals.icu settings. That ends future reads and writes. Because Intervals.icu grants access per application rather than per AI, removing it disconnects every AI you had connected.

To delete your data as well: ask by email — the support page has the steps. Ask the coach to delete everything and it hands you that page; nothing is submitted and nothing removed until you write. Revoke first or write first, either way: your athlete id finds the account.

Workouts already on your Intervals.icu calendar stay yours to keep or remove either way. More detail on the support page.
